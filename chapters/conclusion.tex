\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Discussion}
    \small
    I have presented two frameworks for the shape optimization of a fighter aircraft for aerodynamic performance and low observability.
    \begin{itemize}[label=\ding{227}]
        \onslide<2->
        \item A bidisciplinary gradient-based optimization framework coupling a RANS solver and a low fidelity Physical Optics solver.
        \only<2>
        {
        \begin{itemize}[label=$\bullet$]
            \item The PO+PTD solver is relevant for low fidelity RCS optimization at high frequencies.
            \item The studies showcased show that the framework is able to explore efficiently the parameter space and to identify relevant tradeoffs between aerodynamic performance and RCS reduction.
            \item The stealth criterion that I presented allows to produce shapes representative of real life stealth design.
        \end{itemize}
        }
        \onslide<3->
        \item A high fidelity gradient-based optimization framework relying on an adjoint formulation of a high fidelity BEM solver.
        \begin{itemize}[label=$\bullet$]
            \item The gradient is not accurate because of an omitted term in the adjoint due to limited time to implement it.
            \item The studies showcased show that the framework is able to reduce significantly the RCS of a generic fighter aircraft in a time frame consistent with industrial constraints.
            \item The comparison to a lower fidelity solver shows that the omitted term in the gradient alone does not fully explain the poorer convergence.
            \item Efforts should be carried on to finish the implementation of the adjoint.
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

        \item Optimize and accelerate the BEM adjoint solver.
