\section{Conclusion and perspectives}
\subsection{Conclusion}
\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Conclusion}

    I have presented two frameworks for the shape optimization of fighter aircraft for aerodynamic performance and low observability.
    \begin{itemize}[label=\ding{227}]
        \item<2-> A bidisciplinary gradient based optimization framework coupling a RANS solver and a low fidelity Physical Optics solver.
        \only<3>{
        \begin{itemize}[label=$\bullet$]
            % \item The use of a CAD solver is relevant for an industrial framework.
            \item I presented and validated a PO+PTD solver for the RCS computation.
            \item The PO+PTD solver is relevant for low fidelity RCS optimization at high frequencies.
            \item The studies showcased show that the framework is able explore efficiently the parameter space and to identify relevant trade-offs between aerodynamic performance and RCS reduction.
        \end{itemize}
        }
        \item<2-> A high fidelity gradient based optimization framework relying on an adjoint formulation of a high fidelity BEM solver.
        \only<4>{
        \begin{itemize}[label=$\bullet$]
            \item I presented an adjoint formulation to the BEM solver to compute the gradient of the RCS.
            \item The gradient is not accurate because of an omitted term in the adjoint due to limited time to implement it.
            \item The studies showcased show that the framework is able to reduce significantly the RCS of a generic fighter aircraft at high frequencies in a time fram consistent with industrial constraints.
            \item Thus even with the incomplete adjoint, the framework is useful in an industrial framework.
        \end{itemize}
        }
    \end{itemize}

\end{frame}

\subsection{Perspectives}
\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Perspectives}

    \begin{itemize}[label=$\bullet$]
        \item Complete the adjoint formulation to the BEM solver to have an accurate gradient of the RCS should be the first priority.
        \item Optimize and industrialize the BEM adjoint solver.
        \item Use the high fidelity BEM framework in a bidisciplinary optimization framework coupling aerodynamics and electromagnetism.
        \item Explore multifidelity techniques to couple the low fidelity PO solver and the high fidelity BEM solver in a single optimization framework.
        \item Implement more efficient methods to identify the Pareto front.
    \end{itemize}

    

\end{frame}
