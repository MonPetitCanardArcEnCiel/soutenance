\subsection{Context and objectives}
\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Introduction}
    \begin{itemize}[label=\ding{227}]
        \item<1-> Modern fighter aircraft need aerodynamic performance and low observability.
        \item<2-> These two aspects are generally antagonistic.
        \item<5-> This thesis proposes two multidisciplinary gradient based shape optimization frameworks to improve both aspects simultaneously.
        \begin{itemize}[label=$\bullet$]
            \item<6-> A bidisciplinary gradient framework coupling aerodynamics and electomagnetism with a low fidelity radar scattering solver.
            \begin{itemize}[label=\ding{229}]
                \item<7-> RANS solver for the aerodynamics.
                \item<7-> Physical optics (PO), a low fidelity, high frequency solver for the radar scattering.
            \end{itemize}
            \item<8-> A framework where we derived the gradient of a high fidelity radar cattering solver with an adjoint formulation for a high fidelity gradient based optimization.
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
