\subsection*{Context and objectives}
\begin{frame}[t]
    \frametitle{Introduction}
    \begin{itemize}[label=\ding{227}]
        \item<1-> Modern fighter aircraft need aerodynamic performance and low observability.
        \item<2-> These two aspects are generally antagonistic.
        \item<5-> This thesis proposes two gradient-based shape optimization frameworks to efficiently improve both aspects simultaneously in an industrial process.
        \begin{itemize}[label=$\bullet$]
            \item<6-> A bidisciplinary gradient-based framework coupling aerodynamics and electomagnetism.
            \only<1-9>{
            \begin{itemize}[label=\ding{229}]
                \item<7-> RANS solver for the aerodynamics.
                \item<7-> Physical optics (PO), a low fidelity solver for the radar scattering.
                \item<7-> We showcase this framework by identifying a Pareto front for aerodynamic performance and radar cross section (RCS) reduction on simplified aircraft configuration.
            \end{itemize}
            }
            \item<8-> A high fidelity optimization framework for radar scattering.
            \only<1-9>{
            \begin{itemize}[label=\ding{229}]
                \item<9-> Boundary element method (BEM) solver for the radar scattering.
                \item<9-> An adjoint formulation to the BEM solver to compute the gradient of the RCS.
                \item<9-> We showcase this framework by minimizing the RCS of a generic fighter aircraft at high frequencies.
            \end{itemize}
            }
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
